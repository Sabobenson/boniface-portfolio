01 — Profile

About Me

I am a Telecommunication Systems Engineer with an engineering background from Mbeya University of Science and Technology, and practical exposure in telecommunications and computer networking gained through industrial training and hands-on project work.

My work sits at the intersection of communication infrastructure and computing — from configuring routing and VLANs, to understanding how signals travel across fiber and wireless links, to applying machine learning to real-world problems. I enjoy taking a system apart to understand how it works, then putting it back together more reliably.

I am currently building depth across telecommunication systems, network infrastructure and artificial intelligence, with the long-term goal of contributing to Tanzania's growing ICT and connectivity sector.
